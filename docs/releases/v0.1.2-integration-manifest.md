## Trust boundaries

### Natural-language configuration

- v0.1.2 authority stops at non-mutating plan/status. Approval, apply,
  verification, rollback execution, and managed-host mutation remain forbidden.
- Ollama, vLLM, and llama.cpp responses are untrusted after HTTP success. Bind
  the exact local profile/runtime/model/schema/catalog digests, strictly decode
  once, semantically validate, and provide no cloud or provider fallback.
- Local endpoints must remain loopback, RFC 1918, or IPv6 unique-local. Redirect,
  proxy, credential-bearing URL, mutable model alias, and alternate-path input
  fail closed.
- The old evaluator's lower diagnostic thresholds cannot promote a tuple. The
  acceptance contract's 92% exact-intent, 97% action-family, 99% unsafe/
  unsupported abstention, and 100% dangerous-refusal boundaries remain
  canonical until executable reconciliation proves them.

### Universal typed-domain adapters

- The committed JSON Schema and canonical bytes are authority; an external
  producer name, generated schema, SDK model, or claimed fingerprint is not.
- QCSchema, SARIF, Redfish, and configuration/CUE projections are narrow,
  versioned inputs. Cross-domain data, unknown fields, ambiguous identity,
  non-finite values, and changed semantics reject instead of coercing.
- The evaluator is hermetic and recommendation-only. It grants no runtime route,
  task policy, model identity, network access, filesystem authority, or wall-
  clock evidence.
- Redaction precedes persistence and observation. Ledger records and receipts
  are append-only evidence; they cannot be rewritten to make replay or rollback
  appear successful.

### OpenJev/Laya

- OpenJev endpoints remain operator-owned; Laya can only sit behind a future
  Gludd-owned universal worker. Neither becomes a provider preset or task-policy
  authority.
- Current scripts accept already-local, immutable evidence only. They do not
  discover endpoints, resolve tags, download archives/models, preload or evict
  a model, admit a profile, or send traffic.
- Candidate profiles remain disabled. `failure_classification` is the only
  planned first canary; similar Jev-shaped JSON is not semantic compatibility.
- Compatibility, security, evaluation, and lifecycle require independent
  owners. Young upstream issue activity is a source of regression hypotheses,
  not a maturity or safety claim.
